4 steps to solve / วิธีทำ 4 ขั้น:
  1. Read and find the two numbers. อ่านโจทย์ หาตัวเลข 2 ตัว
  2. Draw a bar model. วาดแผนภาพแท่ง
  3. Write the number sentence and multiply. เขียนประโยคสัญลักษณ์ แล้วคูณ
  4. Answer in a full sentence with the unit. ตอบเป็นประโยค พร้อมหน่วย

🎾 Worked Example 1 — "times as many as"

Mr. Kumar has 8 tennis balls.
Mr. Tay has 3 times as many tennis balls as Mr. Kumar.
How many tennis balls does Mr. Tay have?

Mr Kumar

8

Mr Tay (3 equal parts = ?)

888

8 × 3 = 24

Mr. Tay has 24 tennis balls.

🖊️ Worked Example 2 — "altogether"

There are 12 pens in one packet.
Lisa bought 9 packets of pens.
How many pens did Lisa buy altogether?

1 packet

12

9 packets = ?

1212121212 12121212

12 × 9 = 108

Lisa bought 108 pens altogether.

Working: ones 2×9 = 18 → write 8, carry 1  •  tens 1×9 = 9, +1 = 10 → 108
